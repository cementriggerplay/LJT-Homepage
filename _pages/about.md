## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by Professor Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Education (Academic Background)

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024–Present
  - HKUST NLP Group; Supervisor: Professor Junxian He
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), 2020–2024 (graduated in June 2024)
  - Previously advised by Professor Junxian He during undergraduate studies at SJTU
  - Received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship

## Research Experience

- **Research Intern**, Tencent WXG, June 2024 – September 2024 (Advisor: Zifei Shan)
- **Research Intern**, Shanghai AI Lab, June 2023 – December 2023 (Advisor: Prof. Yu Cheng)
